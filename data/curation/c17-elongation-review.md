# C17 precursor gap: carrier-matched supply and a four-step hypothesis

## Diagnostic evidence

`phase1-odd-chain-supply.json` assesses eleven exact odd-chain acyl-CoA structures, C3 through C23, in the pinned odd-chain model. It preserves the C17 starter's charged, stereospecified CoA tail; no alternate protonation, stereochemistry or ACP carrier is silently joined. Each producing step retains all inputs and coefficients, direction identity and its full underlying reaction record. This diagnostic panel does not replace the 6,220-record metabolite inventory.

Exact net CO₂ conversion certificates were obtained for C3 and C15 acyl-CoA. C13 has no producing equation. C5, C7, C9, C11, C17, C19, C21 and C23 were solver-reported infeasible. Failure status is retained and is not generalized to biological absence. C15 availability provides a specific next gap-filling test rather than justification to import an odd-chain organic precursor.

## Proposed chemistry and complete-inventory result

`phase1-c17-elongation.json` proposes C15→C17 elongation through four equations derived from the Terpedia C18→C20 reference sequence. Only saturated terminal chain length changes; all other bonds, specified stereochemistry, CoA carrier, cofactors, charges and stoichiometry are retained. Four equations are balanced independently and proposed forward-only. Two intermediate structures are new to the model. No target-substrate assay or Cannabis enzyme assignment is claimed.

With those four steps, `phase1-c17-elongation-net.json` retains all 2,729 prior exact structural certificates and obtains four new certificates:

All four certificates use every new C15→C17 step. They are complete net stoichiometric certificates under inherited model assumptions, not merely adjacent graph edges or experimentally established routes. Every internal compound has nonnegative net production, with pre-existing pools permitted only if regenerated. CO₂ remains the sole carbon-containing external input.

The full inventory now has **2,736 conditional covered records / 2,733 structural certificates**, 2,993 missing-producer records, 456 solver-reported infeasible records and 35 exchange-species inventory records. Balanced participation remains 3,219 records. The model has 18,215 balanced equations. Earlier obstruction proofs fail in this expanded model, as expected; they remain valid only for their pinned earlier models.

## What remains unestablished

These pathways still consume protons, iron redox species, iron–sulfur clusters and peroxide under the permissive inorganic boundary. They are not restricted-medium results, proof of photosynthetic energy balance, initial pool synthesis, transport, growth or Cannabis physiology. Their modeled CO₂ totals are net stoichiometric amounts, not measured assimilation yields or a fertilizer prescription.

The reaction-inference skill's exact-identity and all-input requirements determined both the C15 precursor selection and the certificate checks. Next biological tests should establish substrate acceptance for the four elongation steps and distinguish their carrier, compartment and intermediate stereochemistry. The upstream C15 certificate also needs organism/direction/energy review; its mathematical existence does not establish the plant's actual initiation route. Enzyme assignments remain separate from Phase 1 reaction coverage.

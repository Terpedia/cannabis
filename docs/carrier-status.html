The historical model represented some complete protein-bound species using only their reactive groups. This could connect protein-bound iron to free iron and omit the need to regenerate a particular carrier. Arithmetic balance of that projection does not validate the full chemical identities.

Remaining evidence gaps

Thirty-five restored variants lack scaffold definitions, and two have mismatched source carrier contexts. The other 1,305 conserve proposed redox-scaffold counts conditionally, not complete macromolecular formulas. Polymer-only participants, inferred templates, physiological directions, carrier startup and Cannabis-specific activity still need review. Free nutrients must remain separate from carrier-bound groups; a minimum defined medium has not been established.
